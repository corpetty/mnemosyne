## Mnemosyne 0.33.0

### More of Mnemosyne on a Mac
- **Phonon-2 on the Mac.** The best English transcriber now installs on Apple silicon too (Settings → Transcription, or the setup wizard), and runs on the Mac's GPU through Apple's MLX. If that engine does not start, it uses the CPU instead.
- **The built-in summary model runs on the Mac**, on its GPU (Metal). Summaries no longer need Ollama or another provider.
- **Echo cancellation.** The microphone is recorded through macOS's own voice processing, so the other people in a call do not come back through your microphone when you use speakers. This is its first release: tell us how it does.
- **Auto-record** notices a call on the Mac too (Zoom, Teams, a call in the browser and the like).
- **Shortcuts use Cmd**, not Ctrl; Mark is ⇧⌘M, and the hints show Mac symbols.
- **Cmd+Q asks first** while a recording is running, and clicking the Dock icon brings a hidden window back.
- **No more keychain prompts for "python3.13".** The app now keeps the encryption key in its own keychain item (moved over once, the first time it starts).
- Notifications work, guest-notes links can use a Cloudflare tunnel, and the desktop calendar option (a Linux one) is no longer offered.
- Still not on the Mac: noise suppression.

### Voice profiles without NVIDIA or a Hugging Face token
- On a Mac, or a Linux computer without an NVIDIA GPU, Mnemosyne now recognises voices across meetings and names speakers live, with a small speaker model that runs on the CPU (a one-time download of about 27 MB). No Hugging Face token needed.
- It is **On the CPU (ONNX)** under Settings → Transcription → Diarizer, and Automatic picks it when neither Nemotron nor pyannote can run.
- Voices learned with pyannote (with a Hugging Face token) and with the new model are kept apart and never compared: a computer that switches between the two learns your contacts' voices again.
- Going back to 0.32 after this update forgets the voices of meetings recorded since, and your profiles go back to the voices they had before it.

### Fixes
- Recording on a laptop that uses your main computer now goes into the meeting you have open, instead of starting a new meeting and leaving the open one empty.

### Install
The app offers this update itself if you run the AppImage, deb or rpm, or the Mac app. Otherwise download the AppImage (`chmod +x`, then run), the offline AppImage, the `.deb`, the `.rpm` or the `.flatpak` (`flatpak install --user <file>`). On a Mac, download the `.dmg` and drag Mnemosyne to Applications before opening it. Verify downloads with `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS`.
