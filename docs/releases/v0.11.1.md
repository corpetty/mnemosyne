## Mnemosyne 0.11.1

### Recordings made on a phone reach your computer
Recording on the phone page (Settings → Server mode, then scan the pairing code) never sent anything: after "Stop and send" the page stayed on "Recording…" and the recording was lost, with no error on either side. Recordings now upload when you stop, and the meeting appears on your computer and is transcribed there. If sending does fail, the phone now says why. Recordings picked from the phone's own recorder app were not affected. Phones you have already paired stay paired.

### Settings no longer slides the app out of the window
Scrolling past the end of Settings (or moving through the calendar options with the keyboard) could push the whole app up and off the window, leaving a bare strip until you scrolled back. The app now always stays put.

### Install
The app offers this update itself if you run the AppImage, deb or rpm. Otherwise download the AppImage (`chmod +x`, then run), the offline AppImage, the `.deb`, the `.rpm` or the `.flatpak` (`flatpak install --user <file>`). Verify downloads with `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS`.
