## Mnemosyne 0.9.1

### Fixes
- **Recording again into a meeting no longer replaces it.** Recording into a meeting that already had audio (after a crash, a pause or a restart) used to replace its audio, then its transcript and summary. Now it adds a part: the audio is joined onto what is there, only the new part is transcribed, its speakers are matched to the earlier ones by voice (so names you gave carry over), and the summary is redone over the whole meeting. A recording recovered after a crash is added the same way.

### Improvements
- **Stop is always in reach while recording**: a red pill in the header shows the time and a Stop button on every page; click the time to go back to the recording.
- **A roomier recording screen**: once recording starts, the audio sources shrink to one line with their levels, and the live transcript and the copilot sit side by side (on a narrow window, the copilot first and the live transcript right under it).
- **Work calendars without a public ICS address**: choose "This computer's calendars" in Settings → Recording → Calendar to use calendars signed in to GNOME Settings → Online Accounts, for example a Google Workspace calendar. Nothing is signed in to from Mnemosyne itself.
- **Calendar off**: "No calendar" turns off naming, the start banner and calendar-started recordings, and the app no longer checks for a calendar when none is set up. Auto-record has its own switch for starting when a calendar meeting begins.

### Install
The app offers this update itself if you run the AppImage, deb or rpm. Otherwise download the AppImage (`chmod +x`, then run), the offline AppImage, the `.deb`, the `.rpm` or the `.flatpak` (`flatpak install --user <file>`). Verify downloads with `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS`.
