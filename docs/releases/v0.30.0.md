## Mnemosyne 0.30.0

### Live publications
- **The link keeps up.** Tick **Keep the link up to date** when you publish to a host (on by default there) and the published meeting follows the meeting: a re-summarize, an edit of the summary, the notes or a quoted line, a speaker renamed. Within about a minute your computer publishes a new version under the same link, and readers who have the page open see it replace the old one without reloading ("Updated 14:32").
- **Only what you ticked goes out.** Each new version is made from the choices you saved: the same parts, names, hidden identifiers, attendees and quotes. A picture pasted into the notes later is published only once you tick it at the next Update.
- **Readers may comment.** Tick **Readers may comment** and anyone with the link can comment on a decision, a question, an action item, a quote, a passage of the summary or the meeting as a whole, under a name they type. Their comments arrive in your app as "Ana (reader)", marked as from the published link; your replies reach them. Readers see only threads readers started, never yours, with the same names and identifier hiding as the rest of the page.
- **Approve or Hide.** Tick **Approve comments before other readers see them** and a reader's comment reaches the other readers only once you press **Approve**. **Hide** takes any reader comment away from readers; **Show to readers** brings it back.
- **Safe to share.** Updates and comments travel encrypted through a room on the host, which never sees the key. Your updates are signed, so a reader holding the link cannot pass anything off as yours, and a reader's comment can be read only by you until you let it out.
- The dialog shows whether the link is live and how many are reading. A version made by itself is a line in the meeting's history and does not seal a record version; Publish, Update and Unpublish still do.
- Hosted publications only: a "this computer" publication and the one-file export do not update. Your computer must be on for new versions; readers can open the last one without it.
- **A publish host you run yourself** must be updated (`docker compose up -d --build`) for live updates and comments. Until then readers of its links check for a newer version once a minute, and comments are not offered.

### Install
The app offers this update itself if you run the AppImage, deb or rpm. Otherwise download the AppImage (`chmod +x`, then run), the offline AppImage, the `.deb`, the `.rpm` or the `.flatpak` (`flatpak install --user <file>`). Verify downloads with `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS`.
