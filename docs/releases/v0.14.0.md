## Mnemosyne 0.14.0

### Start on any computer
This release is about getting going right away, on the computer you already have, with nothing else to set up.
- **Works without an NVIDIA GPU.** On a laptop without one, setup picks Parakeet for transcription and labels speakers on the CPU. You don't need a Hugging Face account or token for that; the small speaker models download once.
- **Try it on a sample.** The first-run setup has a "Try it on a sample" button: a 70-second meeting excerpt (from the AMI corpus) that is transcribed, labelled and summarized like your own meetings, so you can see what you get before recording.
- **Summaries with nothing else installed.** Choose "Built in" in setup or under Settings → AI and download a model once: Qwen3 4B (2.5 GB), or Qwen3 30B-A3B (18.6 GB) on computers with 32 GB of memory. It runs through llama.cpp on your graphics card (Vulkan: NVIDIA, AMD or Intel) or on the CPU, starts when a summary needs it and stops when idle. On a GPU a 17-minute meeting is summarized in about half a minute; on a CPU expect minutes.
- **Or pull a model into Ollama** from the same card if you already run Ollama.

### A team from one desktop install
- **Share this computer with my team** (Settings → General) makes your computer your team's server, with no separate machine or setup. You become the admin, your meetings stay yours, and the app shows the address others open in Chrome or Edge on your network (with a QR code). Add people under People and send each their invite link; they sign in, record in the browser, and share meetings as on a team server.
- The address uses HTTPS with a certificate your computer makes for itself: browsers warn the first time, and people continue past the warning (Advanced → Accept). If they cannot connect, the card shows the command to open the port in your firewall.
- **Stop sharing** closes the address; accounts and meetings stay.

### Install
The app offers this update itself if you run the AppImage, deb or rpm. Otherwise download the AppImage (`chmod +x`, then run), the offline AppImage, the `.deb`, the `.rpm` or the `.flatpak` (`flatpak install --user <file>`). Verify downloads with `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS`.
