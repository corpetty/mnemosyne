## Mnemosyne 0.25.0

### Guest notes without a host to run
- **Straight to your computer.** Guest notes no longer need a publish host. By default guests reach the notes on your computer over an encrypted iroh connection, through a relay, and the guest page loads from GitHub Pages. Nothing to set up: **Invite guests** works as before.
- **What the panel tells you.** Under the link it says how guests reach you: "Works while this computer is on", and whether that is through a free testing relay, your own relay, or a temporary Cloudflare or Tailscale address. Your computer stays awake while guests can come in.
- **When iroh can't be used**, a tunnel to your computer is opened: Tailscale Funnel if you use Tailscale, else a temporary Cloudflare address (its small tool is downloaded the first time).
- **A publish host is still useful.** With one in Settings → Publishing, it keeps a copy ("Also kept on <host> after the meeting"), so guests can still read the notes after your computer is off.
- **A copy in the guest's browser.** A guest who has opened the notes can reread them, read-only, when your computer is off, until the link expires. The copy stays encrypted, and is deleted once the page learns you stopped sharing.
- **Settings → Publishing → How guests reach the notes** lets you choose: Automatic (recommended), Straight to this computer, A tunnel to this computer, or A publish host; and where the guest page is loaded from.

### The notes editor, live
- **Formatting where you work.** Selecting text brings up a small toolbar for bold, italic, strikethrough, code, links, comments and "Turn into" (headings, lists, tasks, quote, code). The toolbar above the notes is now one slim row. On phones a bar sits above the keyboard.
- **The `/` menu.** Type `/` at the start of a line or after a space for blocks (headings, lists, tasks, quote, code, table) and for inserts that know the meeting. **Insert** in the toolbar and Ctrl+/ open it too.
- **See where everyone is.** Everyone with the notes open, guests included, shows as a coloured caret or selection with their name, and as a dot beside their name. Guests who joined in different ways now see each other too.
- **Moments, people and earlier meetings.** **Time now** inserts the moment of the recording (or where the player is); clicking it opens the transcript there. Type `@` to mention a person and `[[` to link an earlier meeting. In Obsidian they become `12:34`, `[[Ada Lovelace]]` and a link to that meeting's note.
- **Action items straight from the notes.** A task line with a person in it (`/` → **Action item**, or `[ ] ` then `@`) becomes an action item within seconds: it shows in Tasks ("from the notes"), the brief, the weekly digest and Obsidian, and the summary does not list it twice. Ticking it in Tasks ticks the line in the notes, and the other way round. Guests' action items count too.

### Fixes
- **Links in the notes.** Only web and email links can be followed from the notes now.

### Install
The app offers this update itself if you run the AppImage, deb or rpm. Otherwise download the AppImage (`chmod +x`, then run), the offline AppImage, the `.deb`, the `.rpm` or the `.flatpak` (`flatpak install --user <file>`). Verify downloads with `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS`.
