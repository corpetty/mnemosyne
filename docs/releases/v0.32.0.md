## Mnemosyne 0.32.0

### Mnemosyne on a Mac
- **The first Mac app**, for Apple silicon on macOS 15.4 or later. It records your microphone and the call's audio (every app's sound), transcribes and summarizes as on Linux, and takes part in everything else: your devices, people, teams, publishing and live notes.
- The first time you record, macOS asks for the microphone and for System Audio Recording. If the call's audio records only silence, the recording screen says where to look.
- The app is signed, but not yet notarized by Apple: the first time, open it from System Settings → Privacy & Security → **Open Anyway**. Updates then arrive in the app.
- Not on the Mac yet: Phonon-2, the built-in summary model, echo cancellation and noise suppression. Use Ollama or another provider for summaries, and headphones for calls.

### Teammates in the app
- Teammates can join your team from the Mnemosyne app (Linux or Mac), not only from a browser. **People and access** shows an app invite beside the browser link; they paste it in Settings → Devices and people → **Your team**.
- Their app keeps its own library, and the team is one click away: **Your library** / **Team on <your computer>**.
- They choose where a team recording's work is done: on their own computer (the meeting moves to yours once it is transcribed, and leaves theirs) or on yours (streamed as they record). **Put in the team…** moves any meeting of theirs.
- Their app reaches your computer over Mnemosyne's own connection, never your tailnet: they cannot see your other machines or anything but the team. Disabling someone or signing them out everywhere cuts their app off too.
- Teammates in a browser from home: share only this computer with them in Tailscale and allow them the team port alone (docs/team-server.md, "Members from home").

### Smaller things
- Codes for pairing a phone, another computer or library sync, and invites for people, now work for an hour (it was ten minutes), or 15 minutes or a day as you choose.
- Meetings, side views and Settings tabs open at the top; the meeting view is centred, and the recording screen puts the transcript beside the copilot only when there is room.
- The Summary tab keeps its tools in their own row; issue boxes show only when a tracker is set up.

### Install
The app offers this update itself if you run the AppImage, deb or rpm. Otherwise download the AppImage (`chmod +x`, then run), the offline AppImage, the `.deb`, the `.rpm` or the `.flatpak` (`flatpak install --user <file>`). On a Mac, download the `.dmg` and drag Mnemosyne to Applications before opening it. Verify downloads with `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS`.
