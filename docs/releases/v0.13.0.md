## Mnemosyne 0.13.0

### Working together on a team server
0.12.0 gave each person their own meetings on a shared server. This release lets people work on them together.
- **Share a meeting.** The Share button in a meeting's header shares it with everyone on the team or with the people you pick. They can read it, listen to it, export it and tick off its action items; it stays yours to change. Shared meetings show up in their sidebar under your name, and in their search, Ask, Tasks and digests. Sharing and unsharing go into the meeting's history.
- **Shared with the people invited.** A meeting named from your calendar is shared with the teammates invited to it, matched by email (an admin can turn this off).
- **My preferences.** At the top of Settings → General, everyone sets their own summary style and instructions, the words to be alerted about while recording, their calendar's ICS link, their HubSpot email (tasks they send are assigned to them), whether to share every new meeting with the team, and their weekly digest. Blank means the server's setting. The microphone channel of your recordings carries your name.
- **Your tasks.** Tasks opens on "Mine": the action items given to you, by your name or email, in your meetings and in those shared with you. Tick them off right there.
- **Your weekly digest.** Each person gets their own digest, on their own schedule, from the meetings they can read.

### Also
- Summarizing without picking a style now uses the meeting type's style, then the owner's preference, then the server's default. Before, it always used the server's default.
- Tasks on the desktop app has a "Mine" filter too: the items owned by the name on your microphone.
- On a team server, non-admins no longer get "Only an admin can do this" errors when they open Settings.

### Install
The app offers this update itself if you run the AppImage, deb or rpm. Otherwise download the AppImage (`chmod +x`, then run), the offline AppImage, the `.deb`, the `.rpm` or the `.flatpak` (`flatpak install --user <file>`). Verify downloads with `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS`.
