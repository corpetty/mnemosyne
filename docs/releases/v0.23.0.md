## Mnemosyne 0.23.0

### Share meetings with other people's computers
- **Share a meeting with someone, without a server.** **Share** in the meeting's header lists the people you share with; pick what each of them gets: summary, decisions, open questions, action items, chapters, topics, attendees and the notes. The transcript is off by default: once sent it can't be taken back. Audio never leaves your computer.
- **They can take part.** People you share with can comment, tick action items and type in the notes you shared with them. Their computer signs everything they do, and yours checks it before anything is written; the rest of the meeting stays yours. Work done while your computer is off waits on theirs ("Waiting for Corey's computer: 2 comments") and arrives when it is back.
- **Pair with a person once.** Settings → General → **People you share with**: show your invite or paste theirs, then read your fingerprints to each other. Both computers need remote access on and an API token.
- **Shared with me.** Meetings others share with you appear in their own group in the sidebar, marked **Shared by <name>**, with what you may do. They stay out of Ask, semantic search, the weekly digest and Obsidian.
- **Change or stop sharing at any time.** Stopping, or removing a person, takes their copy away the next time their computer checks in; what they already saw can't be recalled.
- **Your own computers stay one person.** Computers paired for library sync share your identity and the people you share with.
- Not available on a team server (it has its own sharing).

### Fixes
- **Summaries with Claude 5 models work again.** With Anthropic's Claude 5 models (Haiku, Sonnet and Opus 5.x), Summarize finished without an error but left the summary empty. When a model really gives no answer, the summary now says why.
- **Rename a meeting from its title.** Click the name in the meeting's header (or ✎): Enter saves, Escape cancels.
- **Library sync:** a newly paired computer can no longer take over another paired computer's sync identity.
- **Remote access:** connections through remote access now always need a token, even when this computer has no API token set.

### Install
The app offers this update itself if you run the AppImage, deb or rpm. Otherwise download the AppImage (`chmod +x`, then run), the offline AppImage, the `.deb`, the `.rpm` or the `.flatpak` (`flatpak install --user <file>`). Verify downloads with `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS`.
