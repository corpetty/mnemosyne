## Mnemosyne 0.31.0

### Devices and people
- **One place for your computers and the people you work with.** Settings → **Devices and people** replaces Server mode, the Remote access box and the pairing steps. **This computer** says what it is: your **Main computer**, a computer that **uses my main computer** (a laptop), or a **Team server**. Below it: **My devices**, **People**, and **Links for people without Mnemosyne**; the rest is under **Advanced**.
- **No setup by hand.** Adding a device or a person sets up the API token and remote access itself.
- **A laptop records, your main computer does the work.** A laptop that uses your main computer streams its microphone and call audio to it while recording: the live transcript, speakers and copilot run on the main computer. If the stream had gaps, the laptop's full recording replaces it before transcription.
- **When the main computer is away**, the laptop keeps its recordings ("2 recordings waiting") and hands them over when it can reach it again.
- The setup asks first whether this is your main computer, and a meeting has one **Share** button: people with Mnemosyne, a live-notes link, a page, or team members.
- **A laptop paired before this version is paired again once.**

### Host a team on your own computer
- Settings → Devices and people → Main computer → **Host a team**. Teammates sign in to your computer from a browser and record there, and your computer stays yours: library sync with your other computers, your laptop, sharing with people, publishing and live notes keep working.
- Teammates' meetings never go to your other computers, your vault, your digest or the people you share with. Ask sees the team meetings shared with you.
- You are the only admin. Reviewers read the team's meetings, and yours only when you share them. Records periods and flagged phrases apply to the team's meetings, not your own; a legal hold holds any meeting.
- A **Team server** (a machine of its own, see docs/team-server.md) works as before.

### Live publications
- Readers may comment on the published **notes** too: they select a passage, and the thread shows against the same words in your live notes.
- **Block** a reader who is being a nuisance: their later comments are refused, and optionally their earlier ones are hidden from other readers. **Unblock** turns comments on again. A reader in a new browser is someone new.
- **Replace the link** cuts every reader off: the publication gets a new link and the old one stops working; send the new one to the readers you still want.

### Records
- A record now covers the notes, the comments, the structured summary and the meeting's details, sealed automatically about a minute after a change. Changes the app made but has not sealed yet show as "being sealed", not as a problem.
- Deleting a meeting's audio (by hand or by retention) is part of the record: the rest still verifies.
- An export can be checked on its own: `mnemosyne-backend records verify <zip>`.

### Safer
- Other computers reach this one through remote access on a port of their own, so they always need their pairing and can never pass for this computer. Before, on a system where that separation failed, they could.
- People you share with no longer count as "everyone" in a team's sharing. Stopping or restarting someone's recording and adding bookmarks need the right to change the meeting.
- A delete synced from your other computer never removes a meeting that records must keep.

### Install
The app offers this update itself if you run the AppImage, deb or rpm. Otherwise download the AppImage (`chmod +x`, then run), the offline AppImage, the `.deb`, the `.rpm` or the `.flatpak` (`flatpak install --user <file>`). Verify downloads with `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS`.
