## Mnemosyne 0.4.0

### New
- **Live speaker labels**: while recording, the live transcript tells voices apart ("Speaker 1", "Speaker 2") and names people whose voice you have saved, updating earlier lines when it recognises someone. Uses the same speaker model as your saved voices, about 10 ms per line on a GPU. Tune it in Settings → Live transcript → "Live speaker separation".
- **Calendar awareness**: paste your calendar's private ICS address (Settings → Calendar). New recordings are named after the meeting in progress, invitees are suggested when you name speakers and exported as `[[links]]`, and a banner offers to record when a meeting starts. Recurring meetings are supported; no sign-in happens inside the app.
- **Out-of-date summaries are flagged**: editing lines or renaming speakers after summarizing marks the summary with a yellow dot and a re-summarize hint.
- **Optional auto-export to Obsidian** after each summary (Settings → Summaries and export; off by default).

### Notes
- Live speaker labels need the GPU/pyannote stack that the app installs when an NVIDIA driver is present; elsewhere the live view keeps channel labels.
- The final transcript after you stop recording is still the authoritative one.

### Install
Download the AppImage (`chmod +x`, then run) or the `.deb`. The backend reinstalls its dependencies once on first launch after upgrading. Verify downloads with `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS`.
