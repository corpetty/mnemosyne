## Mnemosyne 0.9.4

### New
- **Backups.** Settings → General → Backups makes one file with your meetings, their audio and clips, and your settings (not API keys or tokens), to restore on this computer or a new one. Back up now, or every day, week or month, keeping the last few. With encryption on, backups are encrypted too and open elsewhere with your recovery code. Restoring never deletes anything: what was there before is kept in a `pre-restore` folder.
- **Combine meetings.** A meeting's ⋯ menu can combine another meeting into it, for example a call that dropped and was recorded again. Their parts are put in the order they were recorded, speakers are matched by voice (names you gave stay), and notes and clips come along. The same menu adds an audio file as the meeting's next part, and only that part is transcribed.
- **Pair a phone with a one-time code.** The phone QR code no longer carries the server's API token in a plain address. Settings shows a code that works once, for 10 minutes; the phone trades it for its own token, which can only upload recordings and can be removed on its own. An optional phone address (such as a Tailscale HTTPS name) goes first in the QR code; docs/remote-access.md explains reaching Mnemosyne from anywhere with Tailscale.

### Fixes
- **Mnemosyne says when a source stops being recorded.** If a recorder stops, or a source sends no audio for 10 seconds, you get an alert and a desktop notification, and the source is marked on the recording screen. "Restart capture" saves what was recorded and carries on in the same meeting; the live transcript and the copilot's notes stay. (Silence is fine: it still counts as audio.)
- **Quitting during a recording asks first.** Closing the window or choosing Quit in the tray while recording offers to stop, save and quit, or to keep recording in the background, instead of cutting the recording off.
- **Saving an API token in Settings** also gives it to the open window, which was otherwise locked out.

### Install
The app offers this update itself if you run the AppImage, deb or rpm. Otherwise download the AppImage (`chmod +x`, then run), the offline AppImage, the `.deb`, the `.rpm` or the `.flatpak` (`flatpak install --user <file>`). Verify downloads with `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS`.
