## Mnemosyne 0.12.0

### A server for your team
One machine (with an NVIDIA card, ideally) can now run Mnemosyne for a group. People open its address in Chrome or Edge on Windows, Mac or Linux and install nothing. docs/team-server.md walks through the setup: a systemd unit, HTTPS with Tailscale or your own domain, an encryption key sealed to the machine's TPM, and a script that checks the result.
- **Sign-in by invite link.** No passwords: an admin adds people and sends each their own link, which signs in one browser. "Sign out everywhere" and "Disable" take effect at once.
- **Everyone has their own meetings.** Members see and change only their own meetings, in lists, search, Ask, tasks and the live stream. Reviewers can read everyone's, and admins manage people and settings. Someone else's meeting opens read-only, and opening, playing or exporting a meeting is noted in its history.
- **Recording in the browser.** The browser records the microphone, and a call's sound if you share a tab or the screen (only the sound is kept). The audio reaches the server as it is recorded, so the live transcript, copilot and speaker labels work as in the desktop app, and a dropped connection costs seconds, not the meeting.
- **Meetings can stay on the server.** With cloud models off (Settings → AI, or `CLOUD_MODELS=false` in the unit file), OpenAI and Anthropic can't be used at all.

### Also new, on the desktop app too
- **Consent before recording.** Turn on "Ask before every recording" (Settings → Recording) to say how the people in the meeting agreed to be recorded. A short script to read out is shown with it, and the answer goes into the meeting's history.
- **Records.** Edited, re-transcribed or re-summarized meetings keep their earlier versions. Each meeting is sealed with a fingerprint chain that shows when something was changed outside the app. Within a records period you set, deleting a meeting or its audio needs an admin and a reason, and audio clean-up leaves it alone; a legal hold blocks every deletion. Every deletion is logged, and one meeting or a date range can be exported as a zip with checksums (the Record card, or Settings → General → Records).
- **Flagged phrases.** List phrases you want a second look at (Settings → General → Flagged phrases). Lines that use them are flagged, and the meeting waits in the new Review view until someone marks it reviewed with a note. Editing a transcript never removes a flag.
- **Client meetings.** A new `client` summary style also keeps what the client said about their goals, concerns, preferences and situation, and when you meet next.
- **Organizations.** People → Organizations groups the people you meet by company, client or partner. Each organization shows its meetings, what was said and open tasks, and the pre-meeting brief shows what came up last time.
- **HubSpot.** Send a meeting to the contacts' record in HubSpot: the summary, a note and one task per action item. Pushing again updates the same records (set it up in Settings → Notes & sharing). An admin can also bring HubSpot companies in as organizations (People → Organizations → From HubSpot), which only reads from HubSpot.
- **Identifier redaction.** Social Security, account, routing and card numbers and dates of birth said in a meeting are masked in Markdown and Obsidian exports and HubSpot pushes. They can also be masked in stored transcripts (Settings → AI). This works even when a number is spelled out in groups or over several lines.
- **Plainer summaries.** Summaries keep to the words people used and no longer add deadlines, steps or reasons nobody said.

### Fixes (also in 0.11.1)
- The phone page now uploads what it records itself.
- Scrolling to the bottom of Settings no longer slides the whole window up.

### Install
The app offers this update itself if you run the AppImage, deb or rpm. Otherwise download the AppImage (`chmod +x`, then run), the offline AppImage, the `.deb`, the `.rpm` or the `.flatpak` (`flatpak install --user <file>`). Verify downloads with `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS`.
