## Mnemosyne 0.28.0

### Images in the notes
- **Paste a screenshot into the notes.** Paste an image, drop a file, or type `/Image` to pick one. On a phone, `/Image` offers the camera, for a whiteboard. The image sits in the notes where you put it, with a caption under it.
- **Shrunk and cleaned before it is stored.** Your browser shrinks a picture to at most 1600 pixels on its long side and saves it again as WebP (JPEG where WebP is not available). Saving it again also removes EXIF data such as the location a photo was taken. An image may be at most 2 MB after that, and a meeting's images 50 MB together.
- **Guests too.** People with a guest notes link see your images and can add their own, from a phone camera as well. Images in a guest room are encrypted with the link's key like the rest of the room.
- **They go where the meeting goes.** Images travel with library sync to your other computer and with a meeting you share with a contact. They are encrypted at rest when that is on, included in backups and records exports, and kept when you delete a meeting's audio.
- **In publications.** A published meeting carries the notes' images, encrypted. The publish dialog lists them with a checkbox each, all ticked; an image you leave out reads `[image: caption]`. Images and audio clips share a publication's 20 MiB.
- **In Obsidian.** Export writes each image to the folder's `attachments/` and embeds it in the note with its caption.
- **Captions only, for the rest.** Summaries, Ask, search and the MCP server see an image's caption, not the picture.
- An image nothing in the notes has shown for 7 days is deleted. Click an image to see it full size; Backspace on an empty caption removes it.

### Fixes
- A quote's audio clip of a meeting you cannot see now answers "not found" instead of the clip (team servers).

### After you update
- **If you run your own publish host**, update it to this release so guests on it can add and see images. Until then, guest rooms on it keep working without images.

### Install
The app offers this update itself if you run the AppImage, deb or rpm. Otherwise download the AppImage (`chmod +x`, then run), the offline AppImage, the `.deb`, the `.rpm` or the `.flatpak` (`flatpak install --user <file>`). Verify downloads with `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS`.
