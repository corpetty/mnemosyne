## Mnemosyne 0.21.0

### Live shared notes
- **Take notes together, live.** A meeting's notes are one shared document: everyone who has them open (the desktop app, or the web app on a team server) types into it at the same time and sees the others' edits as they happen, with who is here and who is typing. Your cursor stays put while others type; edits made while the connection drops are sent when it comes back.
- **A real editor.** Headings, bullet, numbered and task lists, quotes, code blocks, tables (Tab moves between cells), bold, italic, strikethrough, inline code and links, from the toolbar, the usual shortcuts (Ctrl+B/I/E/K, Ctrl+Alt+1-3, Ctrl+Shift+7/8/9) or by typing `# `, `- `, `1. `, `> `, `[ ] `. Pasting markdown keeps its formatting; copying gives markdown. Undo undoes your own edits.
- **While recording**, the notes sit under the Copilot, and every Copilot line has **+ Add to notes**.
- **The summary reads the notes** the attendees took (the transcript wins where they disagree). Meetings with notes typed earlier get them in their next summary.
- **Comment on the notes:** select text, then **💬 Comment** (Ctrl+Alt+M). The comment highlights the words for everyone, follows edits, says when the words changed or were deleted, and shows up in the Comments card and in search. On a team server, people who can only read a meeting can comment too.
- **On your other computers too:** library sync merges two machines' notes, edits on both included, with no conflicts.
- **On a team server**, a meeting's owner, admins and the people it is shared with edit its notes; everyone else reads them.
- Search, Obsidian export, MCP and combining meetings work with the notes as before. Without the app's sidecar the notes fall back to the plain text editor.

### Install
The app offers this update itself if you run the AppImage, deb or rpm. Otherwise download the AppImage (`chmod +x`, then run), the offline AppImage, the `.deb`, the `.rpm` or the `.flatpak` (`flatpak install --user <file>`). Verify downloads with `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS`.
