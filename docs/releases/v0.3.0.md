## Mnemosyne 0.3.0

### New
- **Ask your meetings** (Ctrl+K or the Ask button): questions answered only from your transcripts and summaries, with numbered citations that open the meeting at the cited line. History is kept.
- **Storage**: see what audio takes space, delete a session's audio while keeping its transcript, summary and notes, or keep audio for N days only (transcribed sessions only, with a preview before deleting).
- **Tray icon and a record shortcut**: Start/Stop recording, Show, Quit from the tray. Bind a desktop shortcut to `mnemosyne --toggle` (also `--start`, `--stop`) to record without opening the window. Selected devices are remembered across restarts.
- **Echo cancellation** on demand via PipeWire's WebRTC canceller, exposed as a virtual "echo cancelled" mic.
- **Server mode**: point the app at a backend on another machine, protected by a bearer token.
- **Background summaries** with an optional "summarize after transcription" setting; untitled sessions are named from the summary.
- **Markdown** rendering for summaries and notes (with an Edit/Preview toggle for notes).

### Fixes
- Notes typed right before switching sessions could be saved into the wrong session.
- Summary pickers no longer flash empty when switching tabs.
- Anthropic models are listed from the API instead of a stale built-in list.

### Install
Download the AppImage (`chmod +x`, then run) or the `.deb`. On first launch the app installs its Python backend with uv (needs internet once; GPU extras only when an NVIDIA driver is present). Verify downloads with `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS`.
