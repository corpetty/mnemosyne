## Mnemosyne 0.22.0

### Publish a meeting
- **Share a meeting with people outside Mnemosyne.** **Publish…** (in the meeting's ⋯ menu) makes a read-only page of the parts you choose: summary, decisions, open questions, action items, chapters, topics, the notes, and quotes with their audio clips. The transcript itself never goes out, only the quotes you pick (**Add to publication** in the transcript's quote bar).
- **You decide what is shown.** Names as they are, first names only, or Person 1, Person 2…; people you untick disappear from the text too. Emails, phone numbers and account numbers are hidden by default. A live preview shows exactly what readers will see. Clips can't be redacted, so listen to them first.
- **Encrypted, with the key in the link.** Readers open `…/p/<id>#<key>` in any browser; the page decrypts in their browser, so whoever stores it only ever holds encrypted data.
- **Where it lives:** on **this computer** (reachable by others when "Share this computer with my team" is on), as **a single file** you send any way you like (optionally with a password), or on a **publish host** you run (`deploy/publish/`).
- **Keep it current or take it down.** **Update** keeps the same link; the meeting shows **Published · v2**, and **behind** once it changed since. **Unpublish** stops the link working (copies someone already opened can't be taken back). Hosted links expire after 90 days by default.
- **On your other computer too:** publications travel with library sync. **On a team server**, an admin turns publishing on; readers see that a meeting is published, not its link. Every publish, update and unpublish is sealed into the meeting's records.
- See docs/publishing.md for what readers and hosts can see.

### Install
The app offers this update itself if you run the AppImage, deb or rpm. Otherwise download the AppImage (`chmod +x`, then run), the offline AppImage, the `.deb`, the `.rpm` or the `.flatpak` (`flatpak install --user <file>`). Verify downloads with `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS`.
