## Mnemosyne 0.29.0

### The copilot and the summary in the notes
- **The copilot writes into the notes.** While you record, each new point, decision, action or question the copilot finds is added once at the end of the meeting's notes, under a **Copilot** heading, each line marked with a small Copilot chip. Anyone can edit or delete those lines; the copilot never adds a line again that someone reworded or deleted.
- **The summary takes its place.** When the meeting is summarized, a **Summary** section (the overview, Decisions, Action items, Open questions) replaces the copilot's lines at the end of the notes. Copilot lines someone edited stay as their notes; your own notes above are never touched. The Summary tab stays as it is.
- **One summary, two places.** Reword a decision, add or delete an item, change an owner or a due date, or edit the overview in the notes' Summary section, and the summary changes with it. Summarizing again keeps those edits. Ticking an action item there ticks the same task everywhere, and a change made in the Summary tab or in Tasks shows up in the notes.
- **No doubles.** A summary action item in the notes stays one task. Obsidian, publishing, combining meetings, MCP and the next summary read your own notes without these sections, so nothing appears twice.
- **Your choice.** Two settings, both on: **Add the copilot's points to the notes as it goes** and **Keep the summary at the end of the notes**. While the copilot adds to the notes itself, its panel no longer shows a button per item.
- Only the computer that recorded the meeting writes these sections. A meeting someone shared with you is left as its owner wrote it.

### Fixes
- An open meeting now shows its summary straight away when it changes in the background, for example after edits come back from your Obsidian vault.

### Install
The app offers this update itself if you run the AppImage, deb or rpm. Otherwise download the AppImage (`chmod +x`, then run), the offline AppImage, the `.deb`, the `.rpm` or the `.flatpak` (`flatpak install --user <file>`). Verify downloads with `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS`.
