## Mnemosyne 0.24.0

### Guest notes: take notes with the people in the meeting
- **A link for the people you're meeting with.** **Invite guests** in the meeting's header (or next to the Copilot while recording) makes a link. Anyone who opens it types into the meeting's live notes from any browser, with no app, account or pairing: copy the link or show its QR code.
- **What guests get.** The live notes, comments on them, and the agenda, with the items the Copilot has seen covered ticked as the meeting goes. Nothing else from the meeting leaves your computer: no transcript, summary or audio.
- **Encrypted, with the key in the link.** The notes are sealed in the browser and in your app; the server relaying them only ever sees encrypted data. The part of the link after `#` is the key and never reaches the server.
- **Who did what.** Guests give a name on their first visit and see that what they type becomes part of your notes. Their edits and comments show in the app as "Ana (guest)"; "Here now" and the notes' status line say who is in.
- **When the meeting is over.** **End guest notes** makes the page read-only, so guests can still read and copy the notes until the link expires (24 hours by default). Guest notes also end by themselves some hours after the recording stops (12 by default, in Settings). **Stop sharing the notes** takes the page down at once; **New link** replaces the link.
- **Where it runs.** Guest notes need a publish host for now (Settings → Publishing, or a separate guest notes host); a public host run by us is planned. On a team server an admin turns guest notes on.
- See docs/guest-notes.md for what guests and the host can see.

### Fixes
- **Stopping a share works again.** In the meeting's **Share** menu, choosing **Stop** for a person closed the menu before you could confirm it.

### Install
The app offers this update itself if you run the AppImage, deb or rpm. Otherwise download the AppImage (`chmod +x`, then run), the offline AppImage, the `.deb`, the `.rpm` or the `.flatpak` (`flatpak install --user <file>`). Verify downloads with `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS`.
