## Mnemosyne 0.7.2

### Fixes
- **AppImage: opening a transcribed meeting could turn the window white.** The AppImage did not include the media plugins the window needs to play audio, and loading the meeting's audio player crashed it. The plugins are now included, the player only loads when you press play or click a timestamp, and if the window's content ever crashes it reloads by itself instead of staying white.
- Fewer harmless warnings in the log during speaker identification.

### Install
The app offers this update itself if you run the AppImage, deb or rpm. Otherwise download it below and verify with `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS`.
