## Mnemosyne 0.20.0

### Comments
- **Comment on anything in a meeting.** A transcript line (or a range of lines from the quote bar), a moment in the audio (**Comment here** in the player), text you select in the summary, or any decision, open question or action item. Reply, edit, delete (no pop-up: click Delete, then Yes), and resolve or reopen a thread; resolved threads fold to one line.
- **Comments stay where they belong.** A comment on a line follows it when the line is edited, split, merged or the meeting is transcribed again; one on a decision or task survives a re-summarize that rewords it; one on summary text finds its words again after a rewrite. If the words changed, the comment says so; if they are gone, it stays, with the text it was about.
- **Where you see them.** A count next to each commented line and item opens its threads in place; a **Comments** card under the summary lists every thread (open, resolved or all) with **Go to**; in **Tasks**, a task's comments from every meeting that mentions it; and the meeting's history.
- **Searchable.** Comments show up in search results (marked "comment"); they are not used by Ask or in summaries.
- **On your other computers too.** Comments travel with library sync and merge like everything else.
- **On a team server**, anyone who can read a meeting can comment; the meeting's owner, an admin or whoever started a thread can resolve it. Writing **@Name** notifies that person if they are online.
- AI assistants connected through MCP see a meeting's open comments.

### Install
The app offers this update itself if you run the AppImage, deb or rpm. Otherwise download the AppImage (`chmod +x`, then run), the offline AppImage, the `.deb`, the `.rpm` or the `.flatpak` (`flatpak install --user <file>`). Verify downloads with `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS`.
