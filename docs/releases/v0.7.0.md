## Mnemosyne 0.7.0

### New
- **Search and Ask by meaning**: besides exact words, search and Ask now find passages by meaning ("spending" finds "costs"), using a small local model (CPU, downloaded once, about 250 MB). Matches found this way are marked "related". Settings → Search and Ask shows the index and can rebuild it.
- **Auto-record**: Mnemosyne notices when a meeting starts, either because an app (Zoom, Teams, Slack, a browser call…) starts using the microphone or because a calendar meeting begins. It can ask you or start recording on its own, and it stops a minute after the app does, after the meeting ends, or after a stretch of silence. Off by default (Settings → Auto-record).
- **People**: a page per person with their meetings, how long they talked, the tasks they own and the decisions made in their meetings. Optionally also a note per person in your Obsidian vault (never over a note you already have).
- **Topics**: follow a subject across meetings as a timeline of what was said, decided and left open, and ask "Where does this stand?".
- **Local-only meetings**: the lock in a meeting's header keeps it away from OpenAI and Anthropic entirely. Optionally, names, emails and phone numbers are hidden from those services in every other request and put back in the replies.
- **Linear, Jira, Slack and Matrix**: action items can become Linear or Jira issues as well as GitHub ones, and follow-ups can be posted to Slack or a Matrix room.
- **Record from a phone**: in server mode, Settings shows a QR code for a small recording page; the recording is sent to your computer and transcribed there.
- **Long meetings**: transcripts too long for the model are summarized in parts and merged, so small local models can summarize a two-hour meeting.
- **Faster first launch**: the app is usable as soon as the basic install finishes; GPU support (NVIDIA) installs in the background and the backend restarts by itself when you are not recording.
- **GPU in the Flatpak**: with an NVIDIA driver, the Flatpak now uses the GPU too.
- **`mnemosyne-bench`** (for tinkerers): measures transcription accuracy, speaker accuracy and speed, against a synthetic clip or a meeting you have corrected.

### Fixes
- An answer in Ask (or a new digest) could disappear if it arrived while the panel was still loading its history.
- Our own recordings are now labelled "Mnemosyne" in PipeWire, so other tools (and auto-record) can tell them apart.

### Notes
- Auto-record starts recordings with the devices you last selected, and only while the app is open.
- Semantic search downloads its model on first use; until then, and if it cannot be downloaded, search works by keywords as before.

### Install
If you run 0.5.0 or later from the AppImage, deb or rpm, the app offers this update itself (deb and rpm installs ask for your password). Otherwise download the AppImage (`chmod +x`, then run), the `.deb`, the `.rpm` or the `.flatpak` (`flatpak install --user <file>`). The backend reinstalls its dependencies once on first launch after upgrading. Verify downloads with `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS`.
