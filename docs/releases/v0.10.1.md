## Mnemosyne 0.10.1

A steadiness release: nothing new to learn, a lot less that can go wrong.

### Recordings are safer
- **Recording into a meeting again while its last part is still saving** waits for the save, and the new part gets the next number. A part whose save failed is saved first; it is never recorded over.
- **Quitting the backend mid-recording closes the recording properly**, and the next start recovers it into its meeting. Before, the recorder could keep running with nobody to save it.
- **Long meetings no longer risk running out of memory.** Mixing streams through ffmpeg instead of loading every source (a 2-hour, 2-source mix now peaks at about 70 MB instead of several GB), and live speaker correction and the live transcript's buffers stay the same size however long the meeting runs.
- A recorder could stall on a long meeting once its unread warnings filled a pipe; that pipe is gone.

### Steadier
- **The app stays responsive while things happen**: looking up audio devices, starting live transcription, decrypting audio, uploading files and resources, and exporting no longer hold up everything else.
- **A damaged settings file no longer stops Mnemosyne from starting.** It is kept as `config.toml.broken`, and only the settings this version cannot use are reset. Anything that fails at startup is shown in the app instead of stopping it.
- **Summaries survive a busy model provider**: rate limits, overloads and dropped connections are retried, and summarizing again after a failure reuses the parts of a long meeting that came back.
- **After a reconnect or a restarted backend** the app catches up: jobs that finished meanwhile are shown as finished (or as interrupted), and a recording the backend no longer has is shown as stopped, instead of spinning forever.
- Turning encryption on or off no longer disturbs search indexing and other work running at the same time.
- Backups skip files deleted while they run.

### Faster
- **Long transcripts open at once**: a 4,000-line meeting shows its first lines immediately and the rest within a fraction of a second, instead of freezing the window for a couple of seconds. Each line's speaker is now a button that opens the speaker menu.
- The Transcript tab no longer asks the backend for its speakers in a loop.

### Smaller things
- Deleting a meeting from the sidebar takes a second click.
- Errors that were silently swallowed now show; notes say whether they were saved, with Try again when not.
- Opening another meeting while recording keeps the live transcript with the recording.
- A finished meeting opens on its summary or transcript, not on the Recording tab.
- Double clicks no longer make two meetings or two summaries; the ⋯ menu closes on Escape or a click elsewhere; dialogs take focus; toasts are fewer, never duplicated, and read out by screen readers.

### Install
The app offers this update itself if you run the AppImage, deb or rpm. Otherwise download the AppImage (`chmod +x`, then run), the offline AppImage, the `.deb`, the `.rpm` or the `.flatpak` (`flatpak install --user <file>`). Verify downloads with `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS`.
