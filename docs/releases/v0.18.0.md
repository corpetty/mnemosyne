## Mnemosyne 0.18.0

### Your edits stay
- **Summarizing again keeps what you changed.** A new summary, a revise or a summary after re-transcribing now merges with your hand edits instead of replacing them: a reworded decision stays reworded, an item you added stays, one you deleted does not come back, and anything you ticked, followed or edited stays even when the new summary drops it. The toast and the meeting's history say how many edits were kept and which lines changed on both sides.
- **Obsidian notes keep your edits.** Exporting a meeting again merges with what you changed in its note instead of overwriting it, and edits to the summary, decisions, open questions and action items in the note flow back into the app (the history says "by Obsidian"). Item lines end with an Obsidian block id (`^m-…`) so they stay matched after rewording; lines you add under an item are kept.
- Without the bundled merge program (a development setup) Mnemosyne asks before summarizing again, as before.

### Fewer phantom lines
- **Clicks and silence no longer become words.** The microphone's keyboard and desk clicks, and quiet stretches, were transcribed as "Yeah.", "Okay." and the like and attributed to you. Lines without detected speech are now dropped from your microphone, live and in the final transcript (on the meeting that showed it: all 34 phantom lines, none of 40 real ones). Settings → **Drop mic lines without speech** turns it off.
- **"Not said" while recording.** A live line that is wrong can be marked with one click (Undo next to it); it is left out of the final transcript.
- **Deleting a transcript line asks nothing.** It is gone at once, with **Undo** for a few seconds.
- Echo removal compares whole words, so "so" is no longer found inside "also".

### One library on two computers (new)
- **Sync with your other computers.** Pair two computers that both run Mnemosyne (Settings → General → Sync with your other computers: invite on one, paste on the other) and meetings recorded on either appear on both. Summaries, notes, tasks and transcripts travel and merge; audio stays on the computer that recorded it and plays on the other through the same encrypted connection while it is reachable. That computer also does the transcribing; elsewhere those buttons say where the audio is. Needs remote access and an API token on both; not available in team mode. New: tried between two app instances in tests, not yet between two real computers, so tell us how it goes.
- New meetings get longer ids (12 characters), so two libraries do not clash.

### Install
The app offers this update itself if you run the AppImage, deb or rpm. Otherwise download the AppImage (`chmod +x`, then run), the offline AppImage, the `.deb`, the `.rpm` or the `.flatpak` (`flatpak install --user <file>`). Verify downloads with `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS`.
