## Mnemosyne 0.10.2

### The window no longer freezes on NVIDIA
On computers with an NVIDIA graphics card, the window could stop updating for good after switching workspaces or moving it to another screen: the meeting kept going underneath, but the window showed the same picture (and black bars when resized) until Mnemosyne was restarted. The window is now drawn without the GPU on NVIDIA machines, which avoids the problem; this interface is light enough that you won't notice the difference. (Setting `WEBKIT_DISABLE_COMPOSITING_MODE=0` yourself turns GPU drawing back on.)

### See what a meeting is made of
- **Parts & history**, on each meeting's Recording tab: every part of the meeting, when it was recorded and for how long, how much time went unrecorded between parts, and the files behind each one.
- **What happened**: a log of every recording started and stopped (and why: you pressed Stop, the capture was restarted, the app closed, the backend shut down), parts saved or failing to save, interrupted and recovered recordings, capture problems, added files, combined meetings, transcriptions and summaries. The log starts with this version; for earlier meetings the parts are worked out from their recordings.
- **Audio that didn't make it in**: if a recording was interrupted and its audio isn't in the meeting yet, the card says so (and how much), opens by itself, and saves it into the meeting with one click. Files a meeting refers to that have gone missing, and stray audio in its folder, are listed too.

### Install
The app offers this update itself if you run the AppImage, deb or rpm. Otherwise download the AppImage (`chmod +x`, then run), the offline AppImage, the `.deb`, the `.rpm` or the `.flatpak` (`flatpak install --user <file>`). Verify downloads with `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS`.
