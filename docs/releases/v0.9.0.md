## Mnemosyne 0.9.0

### New
- **Encrypt your meetings** (Settings → General, off by default): transcripts, summaries, notes, the search index, voice profiles and every recording are encrypted on disk, with the key in your system keyring (it unlocks with your login). You get a recovery code once: keep it somewhere safe, it is the only way back in if the keyring loses the key. Not encrypted: audio while it is being recorded, notes exported to Obsidian, the settings file and the log.
- **Who is who?** After a transcription, one card lists every unnamed speaker with how long they talked, a short sample to listen to and name suggestions (invitees, saved voices). Name them all at once; their voices are recognized next time.
- **Share a quote**: pick a line in the transcript (shift-click another to take a range) and copy it as text with speakers and times, or save that stretch of audio as a clip.
- **Due dates**: "by Friday" in a meeting becomes a date on the action item. Tasks lists deadlines first, overdue in red, with filters for overdue and this week; due dates go to GitHub, Linear, Jira and your Obsidian notes (Tasks plugin format).
- **Better live speaker labels on NVIDIA GPUs**: every 30 seconds the recording so far is run through the same speaker model as the final transcript, and live lines that had the wrong speaker are corrected.
- **Echo cancellation uses the microphone you pick**, shown next to the button and changeable there. It used whatever the system default was, which could be a webcam.
- **Report a problem** (Settings → General): opens a GitHub issue with your description and the diagnostics on the clipboard to paste; nothing is sent until you submit it.
- **Offline installer**: a second AppImage (`…-offline.AppImage`) that installs without any download on first launch. Models (for transcription and search) are still downloaded the first time they are used.

### Fixes
- **Record and Stop react at once.** Stopping used to wait until the whole recording was encoded and mixed, about 20 seconds for a half-hour meeting, with the app unresponsive meanwhile. Capture now stops the moment you click and the saving happens in the background with its progress shown; starting no longer waits for a calendar download.
- **Past meetings show up when the app starts.** The list could stay empty until you did something, when the window opened before the backend was ready.

### Under the hood
- Every release is now also checked by installing the GPU packages (torch, WhisperX, NeMo) on a clean system, and the offline AppImage is tested with all downloads blocked.
- The project's MIT license is now declared in all package metadata.

### Install
If you run 0.5.0 or later from the AppImage, deb or rpm, the app offers this update itself (deb and rpm installs ask for your password). Otherwise download the AppImage (`chmod +x`, then run), the offline AppImage, the `.deb`, the `.rpm` or the `.flatpak` (`flatpak install --user <file>`). The backend reinstalls its dependencies once on first launch after upgrading. Verify downloads with `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS`.
