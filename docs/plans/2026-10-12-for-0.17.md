## 1. Phonon install check in CI

0.16.0 shipped an installer that never worked: the tests' stand-in `phonon` accepted any
arguments. Add `.github/workflows/phonon.yml` (like gpu-extra.yml: ubuntu, uv, Python 3.13) that
runs `scripts/check-phonon.py`: the real `services/phonon_install.py` install into a temp data
dir (the real uv, the real pins), then `PhononTranscriber` on the bundled sample meeting
(backend/mnemosyne/samples/), asserting words came back with times, and that a request without the
API key gets 401. Release `needs:` it. Cache uv's download cache keyed on
phonon-requirements.txt so it is minutes, not a fresh 1 GB each time.

## 2. Phonon pinned by hash

From the review's "not done": `phonon-requirements.txt` gets `--hash=sha256:` lines (generated
with `uv pip compile --generate-hashes` from the same pins, CPU torch from download.pytorch.org)
and the install uses `--require-hashes`. The model file is checked against a pinned SHA-256 after
the install's download and before each server start; a mismatch refuses to start Phonon (Parakeet
keeps working) and says so in the log and the job error.

## 3. Phonon actually used, live too

- `live_transcriber` gets `auto` (new default): Phonon for English when installed, else Parakeet,
  through the same `resolve_transcriber`. Live already runs any transcriber through
  `transcribe(path)` on a short WAV, and Phonon gives live its own server (`threads`); measure a
  tick on a recorded meeting offline (`mnemosyne-bench --live` with Phonon) before changing the
  default.
- The Phonon card: when Phonon is installed but the saved transcriber (or live transcriber) is
  `parakeet`, say so and offer "Use Phonon-2" (sets both to `auto`). No silent migration of a
  saved choice.

## 4. Who "me" is

`mine_matcher` also takes "me", "myself", "I", "you" is not taken. Compound owners ("Jonny Zerah
and Me", "Ann, Bob") are split on "and", "&", "," and "/" and match when any part does. On the
desktop the names are `local_speaker_name` plus its first name. Owners that are speaker labels
follow speaker renames: renaming "Speaker 12" to a name in a meeting renames that owner in its
action items too.

## 5. Track only what is yours

`ActionItem.follow` (bool, kept by `carry_over`). Tasks view: "Mine" (default on every install),
"Following" (others' items you starred: things you wait on) and "Everyone". The open count and
the header badge count Mine + Following. A ☆ on an item follows it; ticking someone else's item
done still works. PATCH `/action-items/{idx}` takes `follow`. Others' items stay in the meeting's
summary, which is the record of the meeting.

## 6. Fewer, sharper action items

- The summary prompt is told who "I"/"me" is (`local_speaker_name`, when set) and to write
  that name as the owner; only explicit commitments, one item per commitment, no restating
  decisions as tasks.
- Copilot to-dos are appended to the summary only when they are mine (or have no owner and
  the meeting is mine); the rest are already in the copilot notes saved with the meeting.

## 7. Obsidian gets checkboxes only for your tasks

`obsidian_task_scope`: "mine" (default) | "all". With "mine", a meeting note's Action Items has
your and followed items as `- [ ]` and others' under "Others" as plain bullets (`- Jonny Zerah:
text`), so vault-wide task queries list only your work. Person notes list their open items as
plain bullets too ("Waiting on" for followed ones as tasks). Settings → Obsidian gets "Export all
meetings again" (a job) to rewrite existing notes in the new form.

## 8. Ticks come back from Obsidian

The vault's checkbox wins over our copy for the meeting notes we wrote: before an export, and
every few minutes while a vault is set, the Action Items of exported meeting notes are read and
`- [x]` / `- [ ]` (and Tasks' `✅ date`) applied to the matching items (same_item). Ticking a task
in the app rewrites that one line in the note (when it exists), so the two never disagree on our
side. Logged in the meeting's history as `task_done` by "Obsidian".

## 9. Links to your vault's own notes

`obsidian_link_notes` (default on): words in the summary, decisions, action items, open questions
and topics that are the exact name of an existing note in the vault become `[[links]]`, once per
section. Case-sensitive, whole words, notes of at least 3 characters; never date-named notes, our
own meeting/people folders, or names that are common lowercase words. The vault scan is the
cached one from people notes.

## 10. Topic notes and a decisions log in the vault

- Topics view: "Save to Obsidian" writes the topic thread with its "Where it stands" to
  `<subfolder>/topics/<Topic>.md` (marker `mnemosyne: topic`, only our own notes rewritten), with
  [[links]] to the meetings.
- `obsidian_decisions_log` (default off): `<subfolder>/Decisions.md`, every meeting's decisions
  by date with a link to the meeting, rewritten on export.

## 11. Ask answers saved to the vault

Ask panel: "Save to Obsidian" on an answer writes `<subfolder>/asks/<date> <question>.md` with the
answer and [[links]] to the meetings it drew on.
