# From an advisory pilot to teams

Corey, 2026-10-01: the firm is not piloting ("private" is not a selling point in that industry).
Keep what is useful to any group sharing a server; drop the pitch. Decisions (same day):
households become organizations; the advisory summary style becomes a generic client style and
the advisor meeting-type pack goes; the "advisor" role becomes "member".

None of the pilot work was released (v0.11.1 has none of it), so names change without
migrations: no config aliases, no users.json or database conversions.

Checks before every commit, as in docs/plans/2026-09-30-advisory-pilot.md. One commit per item.

1. **Pitch material out.** Remove docs/security-overview.md, docs/pilot-checklist.md,
   scripts/make-demo-meeting.py, scripts/docs-to-pdf.mjs. The advisory plan stays as history,
   marked closed.
2. **Team mode.** `firm_mode` becomes `team_mode`: sign-in, the web app, roles. It no longer forces
   consent, supervision or the advisor pack; those are settings of their own. "No cloud models"
   becomes a setting of its own too (`cloud_models`, default on), which the local-only badge
   follows. deploy/firm → deploy/team, docs/firm-server.md → docs/team-server.md,
   scripts/firm-server-check.sh → scripts/team-server-check.sh, FirmPeopleSettings →
   TeamPeopleSettings, `firm_mode` in /health and /api/users/me → `team_mode`.
3. **Roles.** advisor → member (access.py, users, CLI, UI).
4. **Flagged phrases.** Supervision stays as the mechanism (flags, reviews, the Review view, exam
   export); its finance defaults go. `compliance_phrases` → `review_phrases`, empty by default;
   flagging is on when there are phrases (the `supervision` flag goes).
5. **Client style.** `advisory` → `client`: facts of kinds goal, concern, preference, context,
   commitment, next_meeting, other. The advisor meeting-type pack and `advisor_meeting_types` go.
   The "plain words, nothing added" rule applies to every style. Demo replies follow.
6. **Organizations.** Households → organizations (services/organizations.py, `organizations`
   tables, /api/organizations, People > Organizations, `organization` in the brief): people grouped
   by company, their meetings, client facts, open tasks; HubSpot companies sync read-only.
7. **Docs and wording.** CLAUDE.md, api-reference, README; no "firm", "advisor", "household",
   "compliance officer" or "financial" left where a general word fits. Identifier redaction keeps
   its patterns (SSN, account, routing, card, date of birth) under a general name.
