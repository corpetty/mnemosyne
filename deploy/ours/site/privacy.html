Privacy notice

For relay.petty.services and notes.petty.services. Last updated 2026-10-26.

These two servers are run for Mnemosyne, the meeting recorder, by petty.services, the consultancy of Corey Petty. They exist so that Mnemosyne's guest notes and published meeting pages work without anyone depending on a third party's test servers.

What the servers can read

Nothing you write. Guest notes and published meetings are encrypted on the sender's computer before they leave it. The key is in the part of the link after the #, which your browser never sends to a server. The relay only forwards encrypted packets between devices and stores nothing.

What the servers see

  • The encrypted data itself, which we cannot decrypt.
  • Room and publication ids (random), sizes, and times.
  • The IP address of every device that connects, like any website.

What we keep

  • IP addresses sit in the web server and relay logs for at most 7 days, then the logs are deleted automatically.
  • Counts only: we count how many rooms, guest connections, publications and bytes there are, to see whether the service is used and to keep it within its limits. These numbers are not tied to any person or link.
  • No analytics, no cookies, no third-party scripts, no advertising. The pages on this server load nothing from anywhere else.

Guest notes

By default Mnemosyne also copies a meeting's guest notes (encrypted) to this server, so that the link keeps working if the host's computer is off. A room is deleted 24 hours after its last edit, and after 7 days at the latest. The person who started the room can end it earlier.

Published meetings

A published page expires on a date its publisher chooses: 90 days by default, one year at most. Then it is deleted. The publisher can unpublish it at any time, which deletes it at once. A copy someone already opened cannot be recalled.
